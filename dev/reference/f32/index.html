Trigonometric functions

PureLibm.cr_sin — Method
cr_sin(x::Float32)

Correctly-rounded sine of Float32.

Examples

julia> PureLibm.cr_sin(0.0f0)
0.0f0

julia> PureLibm.cr_sin(pi/6f0)
0.5f0

julia> PureLibm.cr_sin(deg2rad(30f0))
0.5f0

julia> PureLibm.cr_sin(pi/2f0)
1.0f0

julia> PureLibm.cr_sin(Inf32)
NaN32

See also: cr_sincos(::Float32), cr_sinpi(::Float32), cr_asin(::Float32)

PureLibm.cr_tan — Method
cr_tan(x::Float32)

Correctly-rounded tangent of Float32.

Examples
